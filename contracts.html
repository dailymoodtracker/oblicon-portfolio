General Provision on Contracts

ART. 1305

Contract

"A contract is a meeting of minds between two persons whereby one binds himself, with respect to the other, to give something or to render some service."

Concept

A contract exists only when there is mutual agreement between at least two parties regarding the subject matter and consideration.

ART. 1318

Essential Requisites Of Contracts

"There is no contract unless the following requisites concur: (1) Consent of the contracting parties; (2) Object certain which is the subject matter of the contract; (3) Cause of the obligation which is established."

Consent

The meeting of the offer and the acceptance upon the thing and the cause which are to constitute the contract.

Object

The subject matter of the contract—the thing, right, or service that is to be delivered or performed.

Cause

The essential reason or purpose why the parties enter into the contract (the "why" behind the agreement).

Three Stages of Contract

Preparation / Negotiation

The period from the time prospective parties show interest up to the point immediately prior to the agreement.

Perfection / Birth

The exact moment when the contract is born through the meeting of the minds (concurrence of offer and acceptance on the object and cause).

Consummation / Death

The final stage where the parties fulfill or perform their respective obligations under the perfected contract.
